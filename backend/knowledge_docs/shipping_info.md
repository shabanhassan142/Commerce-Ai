# CommerceFlow AI — Shipping & Delivery Information

## Delivery Areas

CommerceFlow AI delivers across all major cities in Pakistan including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Sialkot, and Gujranwala. We also deliver to 100+ smaller cities and towns.

## Shipping Options

### Standard Shipping
- **Cost**: Free on orders above PKR 2,000. PKR 150 for smaller orders.
- **Delivery Time**: 3-5 business days for major cities, 5-7 business days for other areas.
- **Tracking**: Full tracking available via order page.

### Express Shipping
- **Cost**: PKR 350 flat rate.
- **Delivery Time**: 1-2 business days for major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad).
- **Availability**: Available for in-stock items only.
- **Cut-off Time**: Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched same day.

### Same-Day Delivery
- **Cost**: PKR 500 flat rate.
- **Availability**: Karachi and Lahore only, for select products.
- **Cut-off Time**: Orders must be placed before 11 AM.

## Order Processing

- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation.
- You will receive an email and SMS notification when your order is shipped.
- A tracking number will be provided via email and visible in your account.

## Delivery Attempts

- Our courier partners will make up to 3 delivery attempts.
- If delivery fails after 3 attempts, the order will be returned to the warehouse.
- You will be notified before each delivery attempt.

## Delivery Instructions

- You can add delivery instructions (gate code, landmark, etc.) at checkout.
- Items can be left at the door if you mark "Safe Drop" in delivery preferences.
- Someone must be present for Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.

## International Shipping

Currently, CommerceFlow AI only ships within Pakistan. International shipping is planned for a future update.

## Lost or Missing Packages

If your package has not arrived within the estimated delivery window:
1. Check your tracking status on the order page.
2. Contact our support team via the AI chatbot.
3. We will investigate with the courier partner within 24 hours.
4. If the package is confirmed lost, a full refund or replacement will be issued.
