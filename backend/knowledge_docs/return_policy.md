# CommerceFlow AI — Return & Refund Policy

## Overview

CommerceFlow AI offers a hassle-free return and refund policy to ensure customer satisfaction. This policy applies to all products purchased through our marketplace.

## Return Window

- **Standard Returns**: Customers can return most items within 7 days of delivery.
- **Extended Returns**: Electronics and appliances have a 15-day return window.
- **Non-Returnable Items**: Perishable goods, intimate apparel, customized products, and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:
1. In its original packaging, unused and undamaged.
2. Accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase.
3. Returned with all tags, labels, and accessories intact.
4. Free from signs of wear, washing, or alteration.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log in to your CommerceFlow AI account.
2. Go to "My Orders" and select the order you wish to return.
3. Click "Request Return" and select a reason.
4. Our team will review your request within 24 hours.
5. Once approved, you will receive a return shipping label.
6. Pack the item securely and drop it off at the nearest courier partner.

## Refund Process

- **Refund Method**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- **Processing Time**: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.
- **Partial Refunds**: If the item shows signs of use or damage, a partial refund may be issued.
- **COD Orders**: For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are credited to your CommerceFlow wallet or bank account.

## Exchange Policy

- Direct exchanges are available for size and color changes within the return window.
- Exchange requests are subject to stock availability.
- No additional shipping charges for the first exchange.

## Damaged or Defective Items

- If you receive a damaged or defective product, report it within 48 hours of delivery.
- Provide photos of the damage along with your return request.
- We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or full refund.
- No restocking fees apply for damaged/defective items.

## Seller-Specific Policies

Some marketplace sellers may have additional return conditions. These will be clearly stated on the product page. In case of conflict, CommerceFlow AI's policy takes precedence.
