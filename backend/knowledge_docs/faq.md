# CommerceFlow AI — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Registration

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click "Sign Up" on the homepage, enter your email, full name, and create a password. You'll receive a verification email to activate your account.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Currently, email changes must be done through our support team. Contact us via the AI chatbot.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact our support team to request account deletion. All personal data will be removed within 30 days per our privacy policy.

## Orders & Payments

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and bank transfers.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Cancel Order." If the order has already been shipped, you'll need to initiate a return after delivery.

**Q: Why was my payment declined?**
A: Payment may be declined due to insufficient funds, incorrect card details, or bank security measures. Try again or use a different payment method. Contact your bank if the issue persists.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: Order modifications (quantity, size, color) can only be made within 1 hour of placing the order. Contact our support for assistance.

**Q: Is Cash on Delivery available everywhere?**
A: COD is available in most areas. Some remote locations may require prepayment. This will be indicated at checkout.

## Products & Pricing

**Q: Are the products on CommerceFlow AI genuine?**
A: Yes. All sellers on our marketplace are verified. We have a strict quality policy and genuine product guarantee.

**Q: Why is the same product priced differently by different sellers?**
A: Sellers set their own prices. We recommend comparing prices, ratings, and reviews before purchasing.

**Q: Can I get a price match?**
A: We currently do not offer price matching. However, you can use promo codes and take advantage of seasonal sales.

**Q: How do I apply a coupon/promo code?**
A: Enter your promo code in the "Coupon Code" field at checkout and click "Apply." The discount will be reflected in your order total.

## Shipping & Delivery

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days for major cities and 5-7 days for other areas. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available in select cities.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Go to "My Orders" in your account, click on the order, and you'll see the tracking status and estimated delivery date.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes can be made before the order is shipped. Contact our support team immediately.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: How do I return a product?**
A: Go to "My Orders," select the order, click "Request Return," choose a reason, and submit. Our team reviews within 24 hours.

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

**Q: What if I received a wrong or damaged product?**
A: Report it within 48 hours via "Request Return." Select "Wrong Item" or "Damaged Product" as the reason. Include photos for faster processing.

**Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?**
A: Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or wrong items. For change-of-mind returns, a PKR 150 return fee applies.

## Loyalty & Rewards

**Q: What is the CommerceFlow Loyalty Program?**
A: Earn loyalty points on every purchase. 1 point = PKR 1 spent. Accumulated points can be redeemed as discounts on future orders.

**Q: How do I check my loyalty points?**
A: Your loyalty points balance is visible on your profile page and at checkout.

**Q: Do loyalty points expire?**
A: Points expire 12 months after earning them if not redeemed.

## Technical Support

**Q: The website/app is not loading. What should I do?**
A: Try clearing your browser cache, updating your app, or using a different browser. If the issue persists, contact support.

**Q: How does the AI chatbot work?**
A: Our AI assistant can help you track orders, check return status, answer product questions, and more. Just type your question naturally — the AI understands context and can look up your account details.
