# CommerceFlow AI — Payment Methods & Security

## Accepted Payment Methods

### Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Pay with cash when your order is delivered.
- Available in most areas across Pakistan.
- Maximum COD limit: PKR 50,000 per order.
- Please keep the exact amount ready for the delivery agent.

### Credit & Debit Cards
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, and UnionPay cards.
- All card transactions are processed through a secure PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway.
- 3D Secure authentication is required for all card payments.

### Mobile Wallets
- **JazzCash**: Link your JazzCash account for quick payments.
- **EasyPaisa**: Pay directly from your EasyPaisa account or app.

### Bank Transfer
- Transfer funds directly to our business account.
- Orders are confirmed after payment verification (usually within 1-2 hours during business hours).

## Payment Security

- All online transactions are encrypted using SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption.
- We never store your full card details on our servers.
- Our payment gateway is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified.
- Suspicious transactions are flagged and may require additional verification.

## Payment Issues

### Payment Failed
If your payment fails:
1. Check that your card details are entered correctly.
2. Ensure sufficient balance in your account.
3. Contact your bank if the issue persists.
4. Try an alternative payment method.

### Double Charged
If you see duplicate charges:
1. Wait 24 hours — temporary holds are often released automatically.
2. If the duplicate charge persists, contact our support team.
3. We will investigate and process a refund within 3-5 business days.

### Refund Timeline
| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Credit/Debit Card | 5-7 business days |
| JazzCash/EasyPaisa | 2-3 business days |
| Bank Transfer | 5-10 business days |
| COD | 3-5 business days (to wallet or bank) |

## Invoicing

- A digital invoice is generated for every order.
- Download invoices from "My Orders" > Order Details > "Download Invoice."
- Invoices include GST/tax breakdown where applicable.
